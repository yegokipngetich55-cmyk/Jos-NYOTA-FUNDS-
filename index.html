About the NYOTA Fund

NYOTA Fund is a national financial empowerment initiative designed to empower Kenyan youth, entrepreneurs and small businesses by providing access to funding opportunities.


The programme supports innovation, employment creation and economic growth across all 47 counties of Kenya.
